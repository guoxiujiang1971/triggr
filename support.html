常见问题（FAQ）

Q：为什么后台收不到围栏提醒？
A：请确认已在系统设置中授予"始终允许"定位权限，并关闭对 Triggr 的省电限制；iOS 上请勿手动上滑关闭 App，让系统管理其生命周期。

Q：如何恢复已购买的订阅？
A：打开 设置 → 订阅 → 点击"恢复购买"；如仍无法恢复，请联系我们。

Q：如何取消订阅？
A：在 iPhone 的「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」中取消，或前往 App Store 订阅管理页面操作。

Q：数据存在哪里？会上传吗？
A：所有数据（场景、事件、录音）均保存在设备本地，无服务器、不上传、不追踪。

Triggr · Support

Thank you for using Triggr. If you encounter any issues, please reach out through the channels below. We usually respond within 1-2 business days.

FAQ

Q: Why don't I receive geofence alerts in the background?
A: Make sure location access is set to "Always" and that Triggr isn't battery-restricted. On iOS, don't swipe the app away manually — let the system manage its lifecycle.

Q: How do I restore a purchase?
A: Go to Settings → Subscription → tap "Restore Purchases". If it still fails, please contact us.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or in the App Store subscription management page.

Q: Where is my data stored? Is it uploaded?
A: All data (scenarios, events, recordings) stays on your device. No servers, no uploads, no tracking.
